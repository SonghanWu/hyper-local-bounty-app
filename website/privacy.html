PinGig – Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

PinGig ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PinGig mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using PinGig, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form) when you register.
  • Location data: your real-time GPS coordinates while the app is in use, to show you nearby gigs and other users. We do not collect location data when the app is closed.
  • Gig content: titles, descriptions, and details of gigs you post or accept.
  • Chat messages: messages exchanged with other users within a gig thread.
  • Wallet activity: records of credits earned, spent, or transferred within the in-app virtual wallet.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and push notification token (to send you relevant alerts).
  • Usage data: app interactions and session data for debugging and improving the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and provide the PinGig service (matching users with nearby gigs).
  • To display your location on the map to other app users while you are active.
  • To send push notifications about gig updates, chat messages, and wallet activity.
  • To process virtual wallet transactions between users.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and safety issues.
  • To improve and debug the App based on aggregated usage patterns.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Other users: your display name and approximate location are visible to nearby users while you are active in the App.
  • Service providers: we use Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting and data storage, all within secure, encrypted infrastructure.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Location Data

Location access is required for the core functionality of PinGig. We collect your location only while the App is open and in use ("foreground" access). Your real-time coordinates are stored temporarily in our cache and are removed when you close the App. Your last known location is retained in our database solely to enable relevant push notifications when you are offline.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, but this will limit App functionality.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account.
  • Real-time location is deleted from our cache when you disconnect from the App.
  • Chat messages and gig records are retained for 90 days after a gig is closed.
  • Wallet transaction records are retained for 7 years for financial record-keeping purposes.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption for all data in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and access controls to protect your personal information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PinGig is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time via device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at team@pin-gig.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
