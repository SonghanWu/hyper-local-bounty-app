How it works
One app.
Two ways to use it.

Every user can post tasks or earn from them — no separate accounts.

🙋
The Requester
You need something done, right now
1

Post your task

Set a title, reward, and location. It goes live on the map instantly.

2

Get matched instantly

A nearby helper accepts. You're notified and can chat to coordinate.

3

Confirm & pay

Mark it done. Payment transfers automatically — no cash, no IOUs.

🏃
The Helper
You're nearby and want to earn
1

See what's around you

Browse tasks on the map within walking distance, sorted by proximity.

2

Accept and go

Tap Accept to claim it. The requester is notified and the chat opens.

3

Complete it. Get paid.

Requester confirms, bounty lands in your wallet. Withdraw anytime.

Features
Everything needed
to get the job done.

From finding someone nearby to getting paid — all in one place.

🗺️

Live Map Discovery

Tasks appear as pins on a live map. New bounties show up instantly for everyone nearby.

💬

Per-Order Private Chat

Every task gets a private chat, auto-opened on acceptance. Coordinate before showing up.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Helpers are pinged when a nearby task is posted. Requesters are notified on acceptance. No constant app-checking required.

💰

Built-in Payments

An in-app wallet handles everything. Funds transfer automatically on completion — no Venmo, no IOUs.

📍

Proximity Enforcement

Drift too far from the task location and you'll get an alert — keeps everyone accountable.

🛡️

Conflict-free Acceptance

Even if dozens tap Accept simultaneously, only one wins. Database-level locking, no double-booking.
